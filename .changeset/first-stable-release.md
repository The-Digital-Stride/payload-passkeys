First stable release. Admins of a Payload `>=3.90 <4` site, on MongoDB, Postgres or SQLite, can sign in to the admin with a passkey (Touch ID, Face ID, Windows Hello, an Android fingerprint, or the device's own PIN or password) alongside their email and password. They add, rename and remove passkeys from the Passkeys section on their account page, and are offered one after a password sign-in. See the README for requirements and setup.
